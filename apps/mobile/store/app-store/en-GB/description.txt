You scroll all day anyway, might as well score some points.

In Quezby the posts come one at a time, and each one wants something different from you. You swipe the ordinary posts up and double-tap a friend's post to like it. When a gold post comes, you hold your thumb down and let go right on the green. And when a red post comes, you don't touch anything. That's the trick, out of habit your thumb always wants to swipe.

The faster you are, the more points you get. The dopamine bar at the top of the screen fills up with every right move and drains with every mistake. Keep going without mistakes and you build a combo, and your points grow. The game speeds up as you go, and the moment the bar runs out, the run is over.

There's a new Daily Feed every day. That day everyone plays the same posts and you only get one try, so be careful. When you're done, you can send your result to friends as coloured squares and see who did better.

In the weekly league you're in a group with players near your level. Your best score of each day adds to your league points, so a run every day works better than sitting down and grinding for one evening. At the end of the week the ones at the top move up a league and the ones at the bottom drop down. You can start in Bronze and go all the way to Diamond.

The leaderboards show the best of today, this week, this month and all time. Follow your friends and you can compare yourself with just them. It even shows how many points you need to pass the player right above you.

No ads. No subscription. No fees. Just fun. Come join us!

The game is in Turkish.
