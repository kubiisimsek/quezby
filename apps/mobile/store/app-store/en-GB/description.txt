You scroll all day anyway, might as well score some points 😄

In Quezby the reels come one at a time. You swipe most of them up, but not all of them. Double-tap a friend's post to like it, hold your thumb on the gold one, and when a red one shows up, don't touch anything. That last one is the hard part, your thumb is used to swiping.

There's a new Daily Feed every day. Everyone plays the same reels and you get one try. Send your score to friends and see who did better.

There's also a weekly league. You're grouped with players near your level and your best score of each day adds to your points, so a run every day helps more than one long evening. The top of the group moves up at the end of the week.

The leaderboards show today, this week, this month and all time, and you can follow friends to compare with just them.

A run takes a few minutes. One a day is plenty 🙂

The game is in Turkish.
